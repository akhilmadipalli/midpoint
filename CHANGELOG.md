## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-14

First public release.

- Three-finger click sends a middle click (hold and drag for a middle-button drag).
- Three-finger tap sends a middle click.
- Menu bar icon with gesture toggles, launch on login, and status.
- Settings window with live finger count, last click, and a debug log.
- Universal build for Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 14 or later.
